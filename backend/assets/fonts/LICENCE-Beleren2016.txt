Beleren 2016 Bold / Beleren 2016 SmallCaps Bold
===============================================

NOT CLEARED FOR REDISTRIBUTION. Resolve before the Milestone 3 client handover.

Beleren is the typeface Wizards of the Coast commissioned for Magic: The Gathering card
names, type lines and power/toughness. It is the reason our composited text can match the
reference site exactly rather than approximately.

It is also not ours to ship. These files came from
https://github.com/saeris/typeface-beleren-bold, whose own README states:

    "The fonts in this library have been extracted from Magic: The Gathering's official
     website, which is owned by Wizards of the Coast. This is not an official or authorized
     library! It is published for non-commercial use and comes with no support."

    "All fonts are copyright of Wizards of the Coast."

That package ships an MIT LICENSE.md. The MIT grant covers Drake Costa's CSS wrapper and
packaging, NOT the typeface — a redistributor cannot license away a third party's copyright.
So there is no licence here that permits putting these .ttf files in a repository handed to
a paying client.

Three ways out, in the order they should be considered:

1. Buy or negotiate a licence for the real face, if Wizards licenses it at all.
2. Ship a cleanly-licensed lookalike and keep Beleren as a local-only development override.
   PlayfairDisplay-Bold.ttf (SIL OFL) is already vendored for exactly this role and was
   chosen against seven candidates on 2026-08-10 — see cards/fonts.py.
3. Commission or draw a display face.

Until one of those happens, `cards.fonts.UNLICENSED` lists these files and
`cards/tests/test_fonts.py::test_every_vendored_face_is_either_licensed_or_flagged` fails
if anything else joins them unnoticed. The list is the handover checklist.
